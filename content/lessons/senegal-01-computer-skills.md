# Technology Education & Computer Skills

> Start with the digital divide, essential computer parts, safe device handling, and the practical technology skills that support communication, learning, and earning.

## Learning objectives

- Explain Digital divide in your own words.
- Apply Technology education to a realistic classroom or community example.
- Complete the practice task and reflect on one improvement.

## Lesson notes

### 1. Digital divide

The gap in access to devices, connectivity, and skills that prevents some communities from benefiting fully from technology.

### 2. Technology education

Learning to use, understand, and create with digital tools so people become confident problem-solvers rather than passive users.

### 3. Computer system

A combination of input devices, processing, storage, output devices, and software working together to complete tasks.

## Guided practice

In pairs, point to each available computer component and explain its input, processing, storage, or output role. Finish by demonstrating a safe startup and shutdown.

Work in pairs when devices are shared. Write your prediction before using a device, then compare the result with what actually happened.

## Check your understanding

- What is the most important idea from this lesson?
- Where could you use it at school, at work, or in your community?
- What would you teach to someone seeing this topic for the first time?
