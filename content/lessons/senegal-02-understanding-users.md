# Understanding a User’s Mind

> Design for real people by considering their intent, expectations, attention, emotions, and prior experience instead of choosing visuals only because they look attractive.

## Learning objectives

- Explain Mental model in your own words.
- Apply User intent to a realistic classroom or community example.
- Complete the practice task and reflect on one improvement.

## Lesson notes

### 1. Mental model

A user’s expectation of how something should work, built from earlier experiences with similar tools and patterns.

### 2. User intent

The real goal a person wants to accomplish when they open a page, application, or service.

### 3. Visual hierarchy

The deliberate use of size, contrast, spacing, and placement to guide attention from the most important content to supporting details.

## Guided practice

Observe two versions of the same screen for ten seconds. Record what you noticed first, what action seemed possible, and which design better supported the user’s goal.

Work in pairs when devices are shared. Write your prediction before using a device, then compare the result with what actually happened.

## Check your understanding

- What is the most important idea from this lesson?
- Where could you use it at school, at work, or in your community?
- What would you teach to someone seeing this topic for the first time?
