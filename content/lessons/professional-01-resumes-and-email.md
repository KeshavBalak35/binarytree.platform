# Resumes & Professional Email

> Build a clear, achievement-focused resume and write professional emails that make a strong first impression for jobs, partnerships, and entrepreneurship.

## Learning objectives

- Explain Targeted resume in your own words.
- Apply Quantified achievement to a realistic classroom or community example.
- Complete the practice task and reflect on one improvement.

## Lesson notes

### 1. Targeted resume

A resume adjusted to match the skills and keywords in one specific opportunity instead of sending the same document everywhere.

### 2. Quantified achievement

A result described with numbers, time, scale, or impact so a reader can understand what changed because of your work.

### 3. Professional email

A concise message with a useful subject, respectful greeting, clear purpose, requested next step, and complete signature.

## Guided practice

Choose a real opportunity. Rewrite one resume bullet with a measurable result, then draft a five-sentence email that introduces yourself and asks for a next step.

Work in pairs when devices are shared. Write your prediction before using a device, then compare the result with what actually happened.
