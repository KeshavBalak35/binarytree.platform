# Networking Safely on Social Media

> Separate personal sharing from professional positioning, use each platform for its strengths, and build relationships through useful, safe, and consistent participation.

## Learning objectives

- Explain Platform fit in your own words.
- Apply Personal brand to a realistic classroom or community example.
- Complete the practice task and reflect on one improvement.

## Lesson notes

### 1. Platform fit

Choosing a social platform because its audience, content format, and norms match the goal you are trying to achieve.

### 2. Personal brand

The credible pattern of skills, values, voice, and proof of work that other people associate with you.

### 3. Safe networking

Building professional relationships without exposing private data, sharing confidential material, or trusting unverified requests.

## Guided practice

Create a two-column plan of content that belongs on a professional profile and content that should remain private. Add one networking message that offers value before asking for help.

Work in pairs when devices are shared. Write your prediction before using a device, then compare the result with what actually happened.
