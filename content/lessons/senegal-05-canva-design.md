# Canva & Digital Design

> Create clear visual communication in Canva by starting from purpose, choosing a strong template, and keeping typography, color, imagery, and branding consistent.

## Learning objectives

- Explain Design purpose in your own words.
- Apply Brand consistency to a realistic classroom or community example.
- Complete the practice task and reflect on one improvement.

## Lesson notes

### 1. Design purpose

The audience, message, and action a visual must support; every design decision should make that purpose easier to understand.

### 2. Brand consistency

Repeated use of a defined logo, color palette, typography, voice, and spacing so materials feel connected and recognizable.

### 3. Template

A reusable starting layout that speeds up production but still needs thoughtful editing to fit the actual audience and message.

## Guided practice

Create a one-page community event poster with one headline, three essential details, one call to action, and a limited palette. Test if a partner understands it in five seconds.

Work in pairs when devices are shared. Write your prediction before using a device, then compare the result with what actually happened.

## Check your understanding

- What is the most important idea from this lesson?
- Where could you use it at school, at work, or in your community?
- What would you teach to someone seeing this topic for the first time?
